Bar = percentile among the other fullbacks, per game with highlights. The line marks the middle (50th percentile). Numbers: his clips per game, then the fullback average.

What these numbers are. Taka's AI tags each player's actions in filmed games and turns them into highlight clips. The figures are clip counts per category: how often Taka's AI flagged an action, not minutes, verified actions, or a rating of ability. One clip can carry several tags, so categories overlap. Players whose clubs film more games, or whose games got Taka's Advanced Analysis, have more clips; per-game numbers soften that but do not remove it.

Per game divides by the number of games that produced highlights for that player. Tap a column heading to sort; tap again to reverse.
