Terms. Records read W-L-T, and H and A mark the home and away team. PPG is points per game (3 for a win, 1 for a tie). Rank is within the conference by PPG, then goal difference, then goals for; the official table may order teams tied on PPG differently. Adjusted GD per game is goal difference per game adjusted for the strength of the opponents played, centred on 0 within each conference. Opponents' PPG is the average PPG of the teams a team has faced. Upset gap is the loser's season PPG minus the winner's, shown only when the lower team won. Kickoff times are local. All games here are within-conference league games, so comparisons across conferences are rough. Pinning a team with ☆ highlights it everywhere and is remembered in this browser only.
